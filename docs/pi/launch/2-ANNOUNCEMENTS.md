## A. Leadership Announcement (email / leadership channel)

**Subject: Launching Datamatics Presentation Intelligence (Govern + Fix) — internal pilot**

Team,

We're launching **Govern + Fix v1**, an internal tool that automatically checks any PowerPoint
against the Datamatics brand standard and corrects the mechanical issues — fonts, brand colors,
template, logo/footer — while keeping the deck fully editable.

**Why it matters:** today the Creative & Brand team manually cleans up decks from across the
organization. Govern + Fix automates the repetitive ~60–70% of that work, freeing senior design
time and ensuring outbound decks are on-brand.

**Validated on our own assets:** on a real IDP customer deck it lifted compliance from 52 to 73,
fixed 373 violations, and preserved 100% editability. It runs entirely inside Datamatics — no data
leaves our environment.

**Rollout:** Creative & Brand first, then Marketing, Pre-Sales, and Sales/Leadership. We'll review
results with you at **Day 30** to decide on the next phase (auto-generation), which is deliberately

## B. Creative Team Announcement (the owners)

**Subject: Govern + Fix is live — your new cleanup co-pilot (and you own the rules)**

Team,

You've been the brand safety net for the whole company. **Govern + Fix v1** is here to take the
*mechanical* load off you — so you spend time on design, not find-and-replace.

**What it does for you:**
- Anyone can run their deck through it first → fewer broken decks land in your queue.
- It auto-fixes fonts → Segoe UI, legacy reds → #C00D0D, normalizes the theme, adds logo/footer.
- It **never** touches layout, hierarchy, or storytelling — that stays your craft.
- Everything stays editable; if a fix would damage a deck, it's rejected and the original returned.

**You own the standard.** The ruleset (fonts, the #C00D0D red, approved greys F2F2F2/D9D9D9, the
white-first balance targets) is *yours*. If something is flagged that shouldn't be — or missed —
tell us and we update the rule.
